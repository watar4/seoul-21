## Prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: transparent sprite atlas for animated blackjack hand signals in a web game.
Create ONE 4-column by 2-row evenly divided sprite sheet, landscape 1536x1024. Every cell is exactly the same size and contains one isolated photorealistic adult RIGHT hand with a short wrist, viewed from a player's above-and-behind perspective. Same person, light warm natural skin, realistic nails, knuckles, tendon shading, anatomically accurate. The back of the hand is predominantly visible. No left hands. Soft light upper left. Transparent background in all cells. No table, no text, no arrows, no grid lines, no props, no detached fingers. Hand fully inside its cell, with generous transparent space around it. Wrist base consistently at horizontal center and 90 percent down each cell. Do not cross cell edges.
Eight poses in reading order:
Top row 1: blackjack hit gesture, index finger pointing forward and curved downward, fingertip lifted slightly as if just before tapping a tabletop, other fingers loosely folded.
Top row 2: same hit hand, fingertip now touching an invisible tabletop, index joint flexed more, wrist same location. This pair must allow animation between raised and contact poses.
Top row 3: blackjack stand gesture, hand flat, palm facing down toward the invisible tabletop, four fingers together pointing away, thumb relaxed, viewed above the back of hand at slight angle.
Top row 4: same flat stand hand with wrist rotated slightly toward pinkie side, fingertips together.
Bottom row 1: double down gesture, only one index finger straight extended, other fingers folded with thumb resting beside, dorsal view.
Bottom row 2: split gesture, index and middle fingers extended apart in V shape, ring and pinkie folded, back of hand visible.
Bottom row 3: surrender gesture, index finger extended forward nearly horizontally close to an invisible table, other fingers bent, dorsal three-quarter view, suitable to move sideways drawing a line.
Bottom row 4: same surrender gesture with index joint flexed slightly and fingertip lower.
High resolution game cutouts, photographic realism, consistent wrist alignment and scale. Real transparent alpha, no checkered background baked in.
